GROWTH & INNOVATION

COMPANY OVERVIEW

A journey of growth, innovation, and commitment to delivering high-quality fiberglass solutions across the UAE and beyond.

2016 – 2018

Company Established

Al Mamoura Fiberglass Products Trading was established in the UAE with a vision to provide innovative, durable, and high-quality fiberglass and construction solutions. The company began by supplying GRP products, waterproofing materials, and construction accessories to the UAE market.

2020 – 2021

Expanding Expertise

With a commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, Al Mamoura expanded its portfolio to include GRP water tanks, epoxy flooring systems, waterproofing solutions, construction materials, and interior decoration products, serving both commercial and residential projects.

2021 – 2023

Building Strong Partnerships

The company strengthened its market presence by forming strategic partnerships with leading international manufacturers, including renowned GRP panel tank suppliers from Malaysia and Korea. This enabled Al Mamoura to deliver premium-quality products backed by reliable technical support.

2023 – 2026

Diversification & Innovation

Expanding beyond traditional supply services, Al Mamoura introduced customized fiberglass fabrication solutions, architectural GRP products, and project-based engineering support, providing clients with complete end-to-end solutions.

TRANSFORMING THE CONCEPT OF POOLS

PREFABRICATED GLASS FIBER REINFORCED SWIMMING POOLS

Our prefabricated fiberglass reinforced pools combine strength, style, and innovation. Designed for quick installation, lasting durability, and low maintenance, they are the perfect blend of functionality and luxury.

DURABILITY

Fiberglass pools are made from strong, non-porous material, making them resistant to algae growth and staining. They also don’t crack or chip as easily as concrete.

LOW MAINTENANCE

Since the surface is smooth, dirt and debris don’t cling to it as much, reducing cleaning frequency and chemical requirements.

INSTALLATION TIME

Fiberglass pools come in pre-formed shells, meaning installation is rapid. Takes as little as a few days compared to weeks for concrete pools.

ENERGY EFFICIENCY

The smooth surface and composite thermal insulation keep water temperature more stable, reducing heating and chilling costs.
